# 尖塔：琐事 1.25.26 / STS2 Things 1.25.26

- 重做活体巨岩的整套战斗动作：躯干、头部、肩膀和胡须会跟着出拳与砸击一起发力，命中有震颤和回弹；举起的拳头改在肩膀内侧蓄力，前臂不再从画面外垂下来。
- 二阶段变身重新编排为「暗中积蓄 → 双眼点燃 → 立起咆哮 → 双拳砸地」；死亡改为捂住核心、扑倒在平台边缘抓住不放，然后滑落下去。
- 抓住玩家后改为持续握拳、呼吸的循环动作，不再冻结在某一帧；受击抽动改为叠加在当前动作上，不会把正在打出的拳往回拽。
- 愤怒形态有自己的压低身形、出手前的颤抖和更重的落地震动。
- 重做放缩巨甲虫的整套动作：撕咬前后仰张颚、抬起前足，飞扑后咬住甩头；触角三连鞭由身体把力传到触角末端，每一击都能分清；重构化时双触角朝玩家刺出共鸣；蜕壳时全身绷紧、甲壳由暗转亮，再猛然压下；死亡时触角先失力，强撑一下才倒地。
- 修复放缩巨甲虫的头部和几块甲壳在动作中相互错开、露出贴图切口的问题；整副甲壳现在一起运动，只有头部和下颚在不露切口的范围内活动。
- 两只 Boss 的打击帧、动作时长、事件和玩法数值都不变；活体巨岩的骨骼文件由 41 MB 缩小到 8.5 MB。
- 小幅上调第二章「深处」的普通怪与自制精英：单发攻击加 1～2 点，多段攻击每击基本不变，生命约加 5%。格挡、成长、控制和活体巨岩不变。
- 一层事件「诡谲之店」的双商人变简单了：每名生命降为原版的三分之一，攻击伤害降低 40%，两人合起来大致相当于一只一层精英。
- 支持游戏 v0.107.1 / v0.111.0。

---

- Rebuilt the Living Megalith's full combat animation set: the torso, head, shoulders and beard now drive and follow every punch and slam, with impact shudder and recoil. Raised fists wind up inside the shoulder line instead of hanging from off-screen elbows.
- The phase change is restaged as a brooding build-up, eyes igniting, a rearing roar and a double-fist slam. The death now clutches the core, collapses onto the platform edge, clings, then slips under.
- Captives are held in a breathing, squeezing loop instead of a frozen frame. The hit flinch is layered on top of the current move, so it no longer pulls an attack back toward rest.
- The enraged form has its own hunched posture, pre-strike tremor and heavier impact shake.
- Rebuilt the Scale Beetle's full animation set: the bite rears back with gaping mandibles and raised forelegs, then pounces and worries its prey; each lash of the three-hit whip travels from the body to the feeler tip and reads as its own strike; the reconstruction thrusts both feelers at the party; the molt strains the whole body while the shell lights up, then clamps down; on death the feelers go limp first and the beetle makes one last failed push.
- Fixed the Scale Beetle's head and armour plates sliding apart mid-animation and showing the cut edges of the art. The carapace now moves as one piece; only the head and mandibles move on their own, within limits that keep the seams covered.
- Hit frames, clip lengths, events and gameplay numbers are unchanged for both bosses. The Living Megalith skeleton file shrinks from 41 MB to 8.5 MB.
- Slightly raised the numbers for regular enemies and custom elites in the Depths (Act 2): single-hit attacks gain 1–2 damage, multi-hit attacks mostly keep their per-hit damage, and HP rises by about 5%. Block, growth, control effects and the Living Megalith are unchanged.
- The Act 1 event "Shifty Shop" is easier: each Fake Merchant now has a third of its normal HP and deals 40% less attack damage, so the pair fights roughly like a single Act 1 elite.
- Supports game versions v0.107.1 / v0.111.0.
